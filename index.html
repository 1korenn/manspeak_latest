    The chair behind the cuts

    MansPeak started with one chair and a simple idea: students deserve cuts that look expensive, without paying like it.

    We learned the craft on classmates and barkada before training properly, and we still cut like it's for a friend. We study the cuts trending on your feed, from K-drama two-blocks to burst-fade mullets, so you never have to explain what "curtains" means.
